Foundry.AI
Corporate Bitcoin Treasury Policy & Founding Resolution

Effective Date: [To be adopted upon incorporation]
Version: 1.0

1. Purpose

This document establishes the foundational policy for the management of a corporate treasury in Bitcoin by Foundry.AI (“the Company”).

The purpose of holding Bitcoin as a treasury reserve asset is to:

Preserve long-term purchasing power in a non-sovereign monetary asset
Diversify corporate treasury holdings beyond fiat currency exposure
Align with the Company’s strategic focus on artificial intelligence, digital infrastructure, and decentralized systems
Enable optional future use of Bitcoin for global payments, settlements, and capital flexibility
2. Treasury Mandate

Foundry.AI hereby authorizes the establishment of a Bitcoin-denominated corporate treasury reserve as part of its broader capital management strategy.

The treasury shall function under the following principles:

Capital Preservation First: Primary objective is long-term value retention
Low-Custodial Risk Exposure: Preference for secure cold storage solutions
Non-Speculative Allocation Discipline: Holdings are strategic reserves, not short-term trading assets
Transparency & Auditability: All holdings must be verifiable and internally auditable
3. Treasury Allocation Policy

The Company may allocate capital to Bitcoin under the following framework:

Initial allocation: determined by board resolution at incorporation
Ongoing allocation range: up to a board-approved percentage of liquid corporate reserves
Rebalancing: permitted only through formal treasury committee approval

No leverage, derivatives, or synthetic exposure shall be used without explicit board authorization.

4. Custody & Security Framework
4.1 Custody Structure

Bitcoin holdings shall be stored using a multi-layer custody model, including:

Cold storage (offline private key storage)
Multi-signature wallets requiring at least 2-of-3 or 3-of-5 approvals
Segregated wallets for operational liquidity vs long-term reserves
4.2 Security Standards
Private keys must never be stored in plaintext on internet-connected devices
Hardware security modules (HSMs) or hardware wallets are required for key signing
Access must be restricted by role-based authorization
Geographic distribution of key shares is recommended for resilience

5. Governance Structure
5.1 Treasury Authority

Control and oversight of the Bitcoin treasury shall be exercised by:

Chief Executive Officer (CEO)
Chief Financial Officer (CFO) or equivalent role
Treasury Committee (if established)
5.2 Approval Requirements

The following actions require formal approval:

Acquisition or disposal of Bitcoin exceeding predefined thresholds
Changes in custody architecture
Movement of funds between cold and hot storage
Any use of Bitcoin for operational expenditures
6. Accounting & Reporting

The Company shall:

Record Bitcoin holdings as a corporate balance sheet asset
Maintain cost basis records for all acquisitions
Track unrealized gains/losses in accordance with applicable accounting standards (IFRS or UK GAAP as applicable)
Conduct internal treasury reconciliation at least quarterly

External audits may be conducted at the discretion of the board or regulatory requirement.

7. Risk Management Policy

The Company recognizes the following risks:

Price volatility risk
Key management/custody failure risk
Regulatory uncertainty
Liquidity constraints in extreme market conditions

Mitigation measures include:

Diversified custody arrangements
Conservative allocation limits
Multi-signature authorization requirements
Periodic security reviews and penetration testing of treasury systems
8. Compliance & Legal Considerations

Foundry.AI shall comply with all applicable laws and regulations in its jurisdiction of operation, including but not limited to:

Anti-money laundering (AML) requirements
Know-your-customer (KYC) obligations where applicable
Tax reporting requirements for digital assets
Corporate disclosure standards

Bitcoin holdings are to be treated as corporate property and are subject to all relevant fiduciary duties.

9. Strategic Rationale Statement

Foundry.AI adopts Bitcoin as a treasury reserve asset in recognition of:

The emergence of digital-native monetary systems
The increasing role of decentralized financial infrastructure
The need for global, non-sovereign capital portability in AI-driven markets

This policy is intended to evolve as the financial ecosystem matures.

10. Amendment Clause

This document may be amended only by:

Board resolution, or
Unanimous consent of authorized treasury signatories (if delegated)

All amendments must be documented and version-controlled.

11. Founding Resolution

By adoption of this document, Foundry.AI formally establishes a Bitcoin treasury framework as a permanent component of its corporate financial architecture.
