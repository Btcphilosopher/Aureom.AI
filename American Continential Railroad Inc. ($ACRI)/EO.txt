# EXECUTIVE ORDER 14XXX

## Recommencing the Issuance of United States Treasury Currency Notes (Greenbacks)

**By the President of the United States of America**

### Section 1. Purpose and Policy.

The strength and resilience of the United States economy depend upon a monetary system capable of supporting commerce, maintaining public confidence, facilitating the settlement of obligations, and serving the financial interests of the American people.

The Constitution and laws of the United States establish the authorities under which the Federal Government issues currency and administers the public finances. It is the policy of this Administration to ensure that the Department of the Treasury makes full and proper use of its lawful currency-issuing powers, while maintaining fiscal responsibility, monetary stability, transparency, and accountability.

Accordingly, this order directs the Secretary of the Treasury to establish a programme for the issuance of United States Treasury currency notes, commonly known as **Greenbacks**, under the authorities and limitations provided by Federal law; to establish the administrative and operational arrangements necessary for their issuance; and to submit proposals to Congress for the expansion of such authority where necessary.

### Sec. 2. Definitions.

For the purposes of this order:

(a) **Greenbacks** means United States currency notes issued by the Secretary of the Treasury pursuant to 31 U.S.C. § 5115 and any subsequent legislation expressly authorising the issuance of additional United States Treasury notes.

(b) **Treasury** means the Department of the Treasury.

(c) **Issuance Programme** means the administrative, financial, security, production, distribution, accounting, and reporting arrangements established pursuant to this order for the lawful issuance of Greenbacks.

### Sec. 3. Establishment of the Treasury Greenback Issuance Programme.

(a) The Secretary of the Treasury shall establish a Treasury Greenback Issuance Programme to exercise the Department's lawful authority to issue United States currency notes.

(b) Within 30 days of the date of this order, the Secretary shall determine:

1. The amount of United States currency notes outstanding and in circulation under applicable law;
2. The remaining lawful capacity, if any, for the issuance of additional notes;
3. The costs, security requirements, production capacity, and distribution arrangements necessary to commence issuance;
4. The accounting procedures required to record and reconcile all notes issued, outstanding, redeemed, or withdrawn; and
5. The legal and operational conditions necessary to ensure that the programme operates consistently with Federal law.

(c) Upon completing the assessment required by subsection (b), and upon determining that all applicable legal, financial, and operational requirements have been satisfied, the Secretary shall commence issuance to the extent permitted by law.

(d) The Secretary shall prescribe the form, denominations, design, security features, and administrative procedures applicable to the notes, consistent with 31 U.S.C. § 5115 and all other applicable laws.

(e) Nothing in this section authorises the issuance of notes in excess of the amount permitted by law.

### Sec. 4. Circulation and Public Use.

(a) The Secretary shall establish appropriate procedures for introducing authorised Greenbacks into circulation through lawful Treasury distribution and disbursement arrangements.

(b) The Secretary shall ensure that all notes issued pursuant to this order are recorded in Treasury accounts and that their issuance and subsequent circulation are subject to appropriate financial controls.

(c) The Secretary shall ensure that Greenbacks are administered in accordance with applicable Federal currency laws, including the legal-tender provisions of 31 U.S.C. § 5103.

(d) Nothing in this order shall be construed to authorise the Treasury to make expenditures without an appropriation, to incur obligations contrary to applicable law, to exceed any statutory public-debt limit, or to exercise powers reserved to Congress or another Federal institution.

(e) No Greenback issued pursuant to this order may be held or used as a reserve in violation of 31 U.S.C. § 5115.

### Sec. 5. Production, Security, and Integrity.

(a) The Secretary shall coordinate with the Bureau of Engraving and Printing and other appropriate Federal departments and agencies to establish secure production, authentication, custody, and distribution procedures.

(b) The Secretary shall prescribe safeguards to prevent counterfeiting, unauthorised reproduction, theft, fraud, and the diversion of notes from authorised channels.

(c) The Treasury shall maintain accurate records identifying the quantities and denominations of notes produced, issued, held, returned, and destroyed.

(d) The Secretary shall ensure that the issuance programme is supported by appropriate internal controls, independent oversight, and regular reconciliation of physical notes and Treasury accounts.

### Sec. 6. Monetary and Financial Coordination.

(a) The Secretary shall consult, as appropriate and consistent with law, with the Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System and other relevant Federal authorities concerning the operational consequences of the programme.

(b) Such consultation shall address the potential implications for currency circulation, payments, financial-system liquidity, inflation, public confidence, and the orderly functioning of financial markets.

(c) Nothing in this order shall be construed to alter the statutory responsibilities or independence of the Federal Reserve System, or to direct the Federal Reserve to purchase, hold, or treat Greenbacks as reserve assets except as authorised by applicable law.

(d) The Secretary shall implement the programme in a manner that promotes financial integrity, clear public accounting, and the orderly administration of the nation's monetary system.

### Sec. 7. Legislative Recommendations for Expanded Issuance.

(a) Within 60 days of the date of this order, the Secretary shall submit to the President a legislative proposal for consideration by Congress concerning the future issuance of United States Treasury currency notes.

(b) The proposal shall address, as appropriate:

1. An amendment to the statutory limit governing United States currency notes;
2. The maximum authorised amount and the conditions under which additional notes may be issued;
3. The purposes for which such notes may be introduced into circulation;
4. The relationship between Treasury currency issuance, Federal appropriations, public-debt management, and the Federal Reserve System;
5. Appropriate accounting, auditing, reporting, and oversight requirements; and
6. Safeguards necessary to maintain monetary stability, public confidence, and the integrity of the United States currency.

(c) Following review by the President, the Secretary shall transmit the proposal to Congress as part of the Administration's legislative programme, consistent with applicable law.

(d) No provision of this order shall be construed to amend, suspend, or supersede a statutory limit on currency issuance. Any expansion of the programme beyond existing statutory authority shall require the enactment of appropriate legislation.

### Sec. 8. Reporting and Transparency.

(a) Within 90 days of the date of this order, the Secretary shall submit a report to the President describing the establishment and initial operation of the programme.

(b) The report shall include:

1. The total amount of notes authorised and issued;
2. The amount outstanding and in circulation;
3. The production and administrative costs of the programme;
4. The controls established to prevent fraud and unauthorised issuance;
5. The programme's compliance with applicable law; and
6. Recommendations for further administrative or legislative action.

(c) Thereafter, the Secretary shall provide quarterly reports to the President during the first year of the programme and such additional reports as the President may direct, consistent with applicable law.

(d) The Secretary shall make appropriate information concerning the programme available to the public, subject to applicable requirements relating to national security, counterfeiting prevention, financial integrity, and the protection of sensitive information.

### Sec. 9. General Provisions.

(a) Nothing in this order shall be construed to impair or otherwise affect:

1. The authority granted by law to an executive department, agency, or its head; or
2. The functions of the Director of the Office of Management and Budget relating to budgetary, administrative, or legislative proposals.

(b) This order shall be implemented consistent with applicable law and subject to the availability of appropriations.

(c) This order is not intended to, and does not, create any right or benefit, substantive or procedural, enforceable at law or in equity by any party against the United States, its departments, agencies, or entities, its officers, employees, or agents, or any other person.

(d) If any provision of this order, or the application of any provision to any person or circumstance, is held invalid, the remainder of this order and its application to other persons or circumstances shall not be affected.

### Sec. 10. Effective Date.

This order is effective immediately upon its signature.
